01 / Publish

From your files to a full artist page.

Albums, singles, artwork, track names, and release dates stay together. Fans get a discography they can explore, rather than a folder of disconnected files.

How to upload and publish your music
  1. Sign in and open Publish My Music.

    Start with the artist upload page. Choose the artist you manage, or create a private artist draft for your first release.

  2. Create a release.

    In the discography uploader, enter the release title and original release date, or mark it unreleased. Add your cover artwork, then choose your audio files. MP3, M4A, WAV, FLAC, OGG, and Opus files are accepted; the uploader shows your current file and storage allowances.

  3. Check the track list and upload.

    Review each song title and track number before starting. Upload one release at a time. If a file fails, retry that file without starting the whole release over.

  4. Complete the rights and licensing step.

    Use the artist publishing flow to provide the rights information and sign the catalog license. You must control the recordings and the music and lyrics needed to distribute them. Draft uploads stay private while the submission is reviewed.

  5. After approval, share your artist page.

    The approved audio becomes available to your audience. Add lyrics and timing, check the public release, then share the artist or album link with your fans.

Open the release uploader →   Read the artist agreement →

Public artist publishing and personal audio uploads are separate. Publish original music you control through the artist rights process. Uploading an audio file to a personal account does not distribute it to everyone.

02 / Add the words

Lyrics that belong beside the record.

Upload a whole album’s lyrics

Open Manage My Artists, find the album, and select Lyrics. Paste the complete album or choose a .txt, .md, or .lrc file.

Put each song title on its own line—1. Title, [Title], ## Title, or the title itself. Choose Split by song, check the song assignments and words, then save. You can also use Add Lyrics or Edit on an individual song.

Sync each line to the song

Choose Add Lyric Syncing. Play the song, then press Space or Stamp when the highlighted line begins. Move through the lyrics line by line.

Use Backspace to undo a stamp, P to pause, and the arrow keys to seek. Preview the result, adjust individual timestamps, and save the sync. For a recording with a different intro, adjust its lyric offset from Versions.

03 / Go anywhere

Your music keeps going when the screen goes dark.

Approved artist-uploaded audio is available to listeners by default for background playback and Apple CarPlay. Fans do not need to upload their own copy of your songs.

On the web

Fans open your link and explore your music, lyrics, and playlists on a phone, tablet, or computer.

In the background

In the mobile app, eligible audio keeps playing when the app is in the background or the screen is locked.

In Apple CarPlay

Use the iPhone app in a CarPlay-compatible car. Approved artist audio can stream directly; saving a copy for offline playback is optional.

CarPlay uses the iPhone app, rather than a browser tab. YouTube-only tracks do not become background or CarPlay audio automatically. Your approved uploaded recordings do.

04 / Understand the listening

See what reaches people.

Your artist dashboard brings together plays, listeners, returning listeners, listening time, hearts, playlist adds, and artist-page visits.

Choose your artist and date range to compare activity over time, then look at individual songs, listening locations, discovery sources, and the moments listeners loved. Open Artist Stats while signed in as the artist or a manager. Refresh to see the latest recorded listening activity.

Listening time appears as timing events are recorded; older plays may not have duration data. Artist stats are available to authorized artists and managers. Listeners have their own account dashboard.

Start a listening room and share its invitation to listen together with friends and fans. Room guests can contribute to the queue according to the host’s settings.
